You can save info about your life, work, or preferences, to get more helpful responses in Gemini Apps, or ask Gemini to look at past chats before responding.

**Important:** We're gradually releasing the ability to reference past chats in the Gemini mobile app, so it might not be available to you just yet.

## Save info & preferences

### What you need

- For now, this feature isn’t available to users under 18.
- For now, this feature can’t be used in Gemini Live conversations. In a text chat, you can ask Gemini to reference a past Gemini Live conversation.
    To use this feature, you must be signed in to Gemini Apps. [Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps](/gemini/answer/13278668).

### How to save info

1. Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. Go to Settings & help   **Personal context**.
  - Or, you can also go to [gemini.google.com/saved-info](https://gemini.google.com/).
3. Click **Add**.
4. Enter what you want Gemini Apps to remember.
5. Click **Submit**.

**Tip:**When you chat with Gemini Apps, you can also ask Gemini to remember info during a conversation. This information will be stored in your

**Personal context**.

[Examples]

- Always give me vegetarian recipes.
- Remember that I don’t own a car.
- Don’t forget that I’m an English teacher.

### Find, edit, or delete saved info

1. Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. Go to Settings & help   **Personal context.**
3. Find the info that you want to view, edit, or delete.
4. Click More .
    
  - To edit info, click Edit .
  - To delete info, click Delete .

### Turn personal context on or off

1. Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. Go to Settings & help   **Personal context**.
3. Next to Info you asked Gemini to Save, turn the setting off or on .

[How Google handles your saved info]

[How to tell if Gemini used your saved info]

Usually, if Personal context is turned on and used by Gemini, you’ll find "Your saved info" in the "Sources and related content" at the bottom of the response. Gemini may occasionally make mistakes and display this label incorrectly.

[Why Gemini can’t save some types of info]

Gemini won’t save info when our systems detect a possible violation of Google's [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms), including the [Prohibited Use Policy](https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy).
